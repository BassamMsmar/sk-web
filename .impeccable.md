## Design Context

### Users
The site serves customers and partners looking for land transport and logistics services in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf, including individuals, private companies, factories, stores, warehouses, and government-adjacent organizations. Their main job is to quickly judge reliability, safety, licensing, fleet capability, and find a direct contact path.

### Brand Personality
Formal, dependable, precise. The interface should feel like an established Saudi logistics company: trustworthy, organized, calm, and capable, with a restrained creative touch that suggests movement and operational discipline.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing green and white identity. Move the visual language toward refined corporate logistics: dark green hero areas, crisp white/off-white content surfaces, geometric route-line motifs, clean cards, strong typography, and simple purposeful animation. Avoid playful, overly colorful, or generic template styling.

### Design Principles
- Keep the brand colors intact and use variation through contrast, spacing, and texture rather than new colors.
- Make the first viewport signal authority, licensing, coverage, and fleet readiness immediately.
- Use motion sparingly: page entrance, gentle floating status badges, and precise hover feedback.
- Keep layouts formal and readable on mobile, with no content overlap or cramped controls.
- Favor clean borders, subtle depth, and logistics-inspired geometry over decorative blobs or busy effects.
